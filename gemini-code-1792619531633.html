🎯 Work Rules (100% Quality & 0% Mistake Policy)
  • 100% Audit Precision: Physical Qty must always be double-checked against System Qty.
  • Zero Scan Errors: Verify WID barcodes on FLO System (10.24.1.53) before marking items as Missing or Short.
  • Mandatory Verification: For INF / IRT resolution, verify whether stock is in transit or in recent putting locations before marking 'Product Not Found'.
  • Picker ID Accountability: Always log the exact Picker ID for same-location issues to ensure accurate RCA tracking.
  • Escalation Rule: Any issue call your team leader immediately.
✨ 5S Standard Guidelines & Descriptions
  • 1S - Sort (Seiri): Remove all unneeded items, empty boxes, and discarded labels from the bin audit zone.
  • 2S - Set in Order (Seiton): Organize WIDs and products neatly inside bins to allow fast, clear scanning without damage.
  • 3S - Shine (Seiso): Keep HHD scanner lenses, trolleys, and bin locations clean and free of dust.
  • 4S - Standardize (Seiketsu): Maintain consistent IMT, RCA, and IRT workflows daily following standard operating procedures.
  • 5S - Sustain (Shitsuke): Continuously follow warehouse disciplines, audits, and safety guidelines without supervision.
🛡️ Workplace Respect & POSH Guidelines
  • Zero Tolerance Policy: Flipkart maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against any form of sexual harassment or discrimination.
  • Professional Environment: Treat every team member, associate, and supervisor with dignity, equality, and respect at all times.
  • POSH Compliance: Any unwelcome behavior, physical or verbal, must be immediately reported to the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).
  • Safe Workplace: Maintain a clean, respectful, and safe working atmosphere for all associates across all warehouse shifts.
