1. W2W (Wall-to-Wall) Manual Audit

↳ Manual physical bin audit across the entire inventory.

2. PST (Perpetual Stocktaking) System Audit

↳ System-driven continuous bin audit to track live stock take .

3. RCA (Root Cause Analysis) Resolution

↳ Discrepancy resolution between physical inventory and WIDs.

4. GTL (Goods Transfer List) Putaway

↳ Transfer and putaway physical stock into designated correct bin locations.

5. TTL (Task Transfer List) Quality & Expiry

↳ System task process to audit, track, and segregate damaged, expired, or near-expiry stock.
